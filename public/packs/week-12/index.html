Week 12 pack — the original that never was

A fixed annual budget, three archive families with real item counts, and two competing preservation proposals computed from stated rates. Compare them, then see what changes when one rate is revised.

  • budget.txt — the budget, the rates, and each family's real item count and size
  • proposals.csv — two proposals' per-family allocation and cost
  • proposal-totals.csv — each proposal's total cost, budget fit and naive value score
  • sensitivity.csv — both proposals rerun after one rate is revised
  • objection.txt — one serious objection to the deep-image proposal, and a response
  • README.txt — what is in this folder
  • reveal/analysis.md — the worked comparison — open it after your own memo
