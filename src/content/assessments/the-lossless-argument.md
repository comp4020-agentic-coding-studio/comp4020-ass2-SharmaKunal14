> Name one thing that should be copied without loss. Price it.

Due **12:00, Monday 7 June 2027** (Canberra time). About 16–20 hours.

## What you submit

A 2,500-word argued position, a costing table or spreadsheet, and a source list.

Say what **preserved** means for your object: exact bits, legible text, audible
content, documented provenance, or another property someone could test. The word
does different work in each case, and the costing follows from which you choose.

## Required elements

- Scope and time horizon.
- A comparison with one alternative.
- Evaluation of at least one restoration or preservation claim, using the
  evidence standards from weeks 10 and 11.
- Storage, labour and redundancy assumptions, using the week 8 overhead framework.
- A simple sensitivity analysis: what changes when one major assumption changes.

Label any fictional budget rates and keep them separate from sourced estimates.

## How it is marked

A pass presents a coherent proposal with basic evidence and costing. A strong
response tests its assumptions and compares credible options. An excellent
response makes the uncertainty consequential to the decision.

**A justified decision not to preserve something is allowed** and can score
highly. A dramatic position is not rewarded for sounding uncompromising.
