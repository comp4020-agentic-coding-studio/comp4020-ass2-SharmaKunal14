> You are given a set of undocumented witnesses from one documented source
> family. Reconstruct the family tree. Then argue against it.

Due **12:00, Friday 30 April 2027** (Canberra time). About 10–12 hours.
The curated pack is released when the week 6 workshop ends, at 14:00 on
Wednesday 31 March 2027; this brief is available from the first day of the course.

## What you submit

A proposed tree or partial graph, an evidence table mapping each inferred edge
to the shared feature that justifies it, a supported partial archetype with gaps
explicitly marked, and a 600-word argument for the strongest alternative tree.

## How it is marked

Your reasoning is marked against the evidence in the pack, not against
similarity to a hidden answer. **"The evidence does not resolve this edge" can
earn full credit** where the evidence genuinely does not resolve it.

The pack's true source and production log are released after the assessment
closes, as feedback. A stemma is a family tree of copies, built from the
mistakes they share; week 6 teaches the method and its limits.
