> Here is some evidence. Say what it supports, and say how you know.

The first five minutes of every Wednesday workshop, weeks 1 to 12.
The date above is when the **series ends** — the final check on Wednesday
19 May 2027 — not a separate submission deadline.

## How it works

Each check gives you prepared evidence and one question. You will never be asked
to complete an experiment in five minutes.

Scoring is 0–4: **2 points** for citing relevant evidence, **1 point** for a
defensible interpretation of it, **1 point** for naming a material limitation or
an alternative your answer does not rule out.

A correct guess with no evidence earns at most the interpretation point. A wrong
final choice does not erase valid reasoning. An indicative answer and explanation
are published after each check.

## Best ten of twelve

Your best ten scores count, so two missed checks cost nothing. An uncompleted
check scores zero before the lowest two are dropped. Approved absences beyond
that are handled under the [course policies](/policies/), which also cover
extensions, late work and declaration of tools.

Weeks 1 to 9 mostly exercise evidence reasoning; weeks 10 to 12 add evaluation
of preservation and restoration claims.
