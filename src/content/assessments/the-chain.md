> Choose a copying process. Put one artefact through ten documented steps of it,
> and report what each step changed.

Due **12:00, Friday 26 March 2027** (Canberra time). About 8–10 hours.

## What you submit

The original and ten outputs, a settings log, a measurement table, the prediction
you recorded before you started, and a 600-word analysis.

Keep the original. Include a control that isolates something your measurement
might otherwise be blamed on. A finding of **no measurable change** is a valid
result and is marked as one — the exact-copy control in week 1 exists to make
that point early.

You may use a process demonstrated in a workshop, provided you pose your own
comparison. You are not required to invent a new technology or acquire equipment,
and no premium is awarded for expensive apparatus or dramatic degradation.

## The prediction

Record what you expect to lose before you run anything. The mark rewards
comparing that expectation honestly with what happened. A prediction quietly
revised after seeing the result scores nothing on that criterion.
