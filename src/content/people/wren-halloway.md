Halloway's position is that damage is informative: the changes a copy carries are
often the most reliable evidence available about where it came from. She teaches
the reconstruction weeks and marks hardest on the gap between what a student
observed and what they inferred from it.

She does not think every question has an answer. A workshop conclusion of
"the evidence does not separate these two histories" is the result she is
happiest to see defended well.
