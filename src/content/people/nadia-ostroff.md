---
title: Nadia Ostroff
description:
  Guest teacher for weeks 8 and 11, on the cost of redundancy and the limits of
  what a provenance record can establish.
affiliation: Slop University School of Invented Disciplines
role: guest
contact: Present in the weeks 8 and 11 workshops.
---

Ostroff arrives when the course needs someone who can price things. Week 8 is
hers: redundancy as a purchase, with the overhead stated in bits rather than in
good intentions. She returns in week 11 to explain what a provenance manifest
actually asserts, and what it quietly omits.

Her standing objection to the rest of the course is that a beautifully argued
preservation proposal with no costing is not a proposal.
