## 2. Weekly teaching brief

Use week 6 dates from the [teaching index](../course-plan.md#6-twelve-dated-teaching-weeks)
and the agreed [clock timetable](../course-plan.md#4-timetable-and-vocabulary).

- Question: Which family tree does the evidence support, and what remains unknown?
- Target: justify proposed edges and compare at least one competing history.
- Activity: reconstruct week 5's witnesses, distinguish observations from
  inferred parents, then inspect the source and parent log. Include a case with
  independently repeated error or copying from two sources.
- Output: proposed tree, competing explanation and post-reveal correction note.
  This method, rather than peer submission quality, prepares The Stemma.
- Check: choose between two trees using a supplied error table; identify one
  unresolved edge. Feedback explains assumptions and alternatives.
- Candidate readings: Paul Maas, *Textual Criticism*; Barbrook and colleagues,
  “The phylogeny of The Canterbury Tales.” Verify exact bibliographic details.
- Accessible route: textual edge list and evidence table alongside every diagram.
  **Release The Stemma's curated pack at the end of this workshop, following course-plan §7.**

**Break: week commencing 2027-04-05. No lecture, workshop or weekly check.**

## 3. How the lecture and workshop run

### Monday lecture — 60 minutes

Build a supported candidate tree, test an alternative and explain an unresolved edge. Present shared errors as evidence under assumptions. Use the worked deck specified in §6 below; distinguish inference from the production log.

See the [shared lecture procedure](README.md#shared-teaching-and-page-procedure).

### Wednesday workshop — 120 minutes

**Investigation (40 minutes):** Construct a candidate graph (20 minutes); justify and challenge selected edges (10); state the strongest alternative and one unresolved relationship (10). Evaluate before revealing the true log; compare against it during Deposit.

Use the workshop stages and quiz rubric from the
[shared procedure](README.md#shared-teaching-and-page-procedure).
