## 1. Purpose and progression

**Distinct capability:** Allocate a limited budget and defend the accepted loss.

**Archive placement:** Cross-family preservation decision. Compare multiple archive families without merging their ancestry. Use week 8 overhead, week 10 evidence and week 11 provenance to justify a preservation decision.

## 2. Weekly teaching brief

Use week 12 dates from the [teaching index](../course-plan.md#6-twelve-dated-teaching-weeks)
and the agreed [clock timetable](../course-plan.md#4-timetable-and-vocabulary).

- Question: What should this archive preserve, given a limited budget?
- Target: defend a choice while acknowledging a worthwhile alternative.
- Activity: allocate a stated fictional budget across at least two archive
  families; compare preserving source files, transformation logs and examples.
  Use week 8's overhead and week 11's provenance requirements where relevant.
- Output: preservation memo and response to an objection. No copying operation
  or numerical measurement is forced onto a deliberative activity.
- Check: choose between two supplied preservation proposals and justify the
  accepted loss. Feedback allows different well-supported choices.
- Candidate reading: a selected Benjamin passage plus an accessible preservation
  case, with editions and claims verified. Keep context tied to the decision.
- Accessible route: budget table and written participation alternative.
  **The Lossless Argument due Monday 2027-06-07, 12:00 Canberra time.**

## 3. How the lecture and workshop run

### Monday lecture — 60 minutes

Define the preservation property and time horizon, then compare two choices with different consequences. Tie any historical reading directly to that decision; do not assume there is one correct position.

See the [shared lecture procedure](README.md#shared-teaching-and-page-procedure).

### Wednesday workshop — 120 minutes

**Investigation (40 minutes):** Allocate a stated budget across at least two archive families (15 minutes); test the choice after changing a major cost assumption (15); respond to a serious alternative proposal (10).

Use the workshop stages and quiz rubric from the
[shared procedure](README.md#shared-teaching-and-page-procedure).
